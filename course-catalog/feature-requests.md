# Feature requests: course catalog tool (fictional)

01. (Student) I want to see all courses in one department without knowing
    the course codes.
02. (Advisor) The planner should warn when a student puts a course in the
    same term as its prerequisite.
03. (Student) Let me print or save my plan so I can bring it to advising.
04. (Advisor) Search should find "biology" even when I don't capitalize it.
05. (Student) Show me how many credits I'm taking each term.
06. (Registrar) Some courses listed were retired two years ago. Who updates
    this?
07. (Department chair) Students can't find our courses; can we filter by
    department and level?
08. (Advisor) I'd like students to send me their plan before our meeting.
09. (Student) Tell me which courses I can take next, based on what I've
    already done.
10. (Student) Can I search by topic, like "climate", not just by title?
11. (Registrar) We need to know when course data was last updated.
12. (Student) Can I just register for classes from here?
