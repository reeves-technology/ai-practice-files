# Approved answers (fictional, for training only)

## Rooms
- Book rooms through the room request form. We confirm within one working
  day.
- Rooms for more than 25 people: the Main Hall seminar rooms. All have a
  projector and screen.
- Same-day or next-day requests: we try, but can't guarantee a room. Say
  we'll confirm by end of day.

## Mileage
- Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at $0.67 a mile (travel policy
  2.2). Submit a map showing the route.
- Submit claims within 60 days of returning, through the expense system
  (travel policy 5.1).

## Hotels
- Up to $200 a night in the US, or the conference hotel rate if higher
  (travel policy 3.1). Keep the itemized hotel receipt.
