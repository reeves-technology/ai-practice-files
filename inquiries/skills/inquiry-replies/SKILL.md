# Inquiry replies

1. Read each email and note its topic and anything urgent ("today", "ASAP").
2. Check routing.md first. If another office handles it, reply with who
   to contact instead of answering.
3. Answer only from faq.md and the travel policy. If the answer isn't
   there, don't guess: list it under "Needs a person".
4. Never promise money, a room or a fix. Say what happens next and who
   confirms it.
5. Follow reply-template.md: first name, three sentences or fewer, the
   next step, signed "Operations Office".
6. Save one draft per email in drafts/. Never send. List urgent ones first.
