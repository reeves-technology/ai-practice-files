# Requirements: online survey tool (example)

Fictional, for training only. This is what requirements.md might look like
after the grill step in Appendix B, example B2. Yours will differ.

Office: Operations Office
Written: October 2026, after a 10-question interview
Status: approved by the office manager on October 22, 2026

## What we need it for

About 20 surveys a year: event feedback, room and service feedback, and two
or three surveys a year of students who used our services.

## Must have

1. **Approved for our data.** Some surveys collect student IDs alongside
   service use, which is Restricted data. The tool must be approved for
   Restricted data on the approved-tools list, or we use two tools: one for
   student surveys, one for everything else.
2. **Campus sign-in.** Staff sign in with their campus account. No shared
   passwords.
3. **Accessible to respondents.** Works with screen readers and keyboard
   only; the vendor publishes a WCAG 2.1 AA statement.
4. **Branching.** Skip questions based on earlier answers.
5. **Export to CSV or Excel,** with one row per response.
6. **Anonymous option** for staff feedback surveys.
7. **Three staff editors** who can share surveys with each other.

## Nice to have

- Scheduled reminders to people who haven't responded.
- Built-in charts we can paste into reports.
- Survey templates we can reuse each year.

## Limits

- **Budget:** under $1,500 a year for the office. A tool with no cost to
  departments is preferred.
- **Responses:** up to about 2,000 per survey.
- **Timeline:** in use by January 2027. A tool that needs a new IT security
  review (four to six weeks) is only an option if the review is requested
  by mid-November.

## Out of scope

- Research surveys that need IRB approval. Faculty handle those.
- Paper surveys.

## How we'll compare

Score each option 0 (no), 1 (partly) or 2 (yes) on each must-have, with a
link and date for every score. Anything not on the approved-tools list is
noted as "needs a review" rather than ranked alongside approved tools.
